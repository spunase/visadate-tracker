# Example Product Brief

## Product name
Visa Date Tracker

## One-sentence description
A mobile-first tracker that helps employment-based U.S. immigration applicants monitor visa bulletin movement and understand what actions to prepare as their priority date approaches.

## Primary user
Indian EB2 / EB3 applicants in the U.S.

## Main problem to solve
Users manually scan monthly bulletins and struggle to understand whether dates are advancing, regressing, or nearing their own priority date.

## Primary action on the first screen
Instantly compare their priority date against the latest bulletin status.

## Secondary actions
- view movement trend
- understand milestones
- read checklist guidance
- track by category

## Key screens
- dashboard
- trend detail
- milestone / checklist
- bulletin update detail

## Emotional tone
Trustworthy, calm, clear, premium, not bureaucratic

## Technical stack
Next.js, React, Tailwind, Supabase

## Constraints
- mobile-first
- no personally identifiable information
- should feel modern and reassuring
- highly readable on small screens

## Reference usage notes
Borrow:
- spacing rhythm
- typography hierarchy
- premium surface treatment
- restrained accent usage
Do not copy:
- fake analytics widgets
- irrelevant illustrations
